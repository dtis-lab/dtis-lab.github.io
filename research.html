Research Areas & Projects

Our lab investigates foundational challenges across modern distributed systems. Here is an overview of our ongoing investigations.

Core Pillar

Decentralized Digital Identity & Verifiable Credentials

Investigating self-sovereign identity models that give users absolute control over their personal data attributes while maintaining cryptographic integrity and verifiable proof validation.

Core Pillar

Applied Cryptography & Zero-Knowledge Frameworks

Designing efficient multi-party computation protocols and zero-knowledge architectures to verify system transactions and data authenticity without exposing sensitive underlying payloads.

Core Pillar

Infrastructure Threat Modeling & System Resilience

Analyzing trust boundaries across complex cloud-edge environments to anticipate failure modes, map threat vectors, and reinforce automated remediation protocols.
