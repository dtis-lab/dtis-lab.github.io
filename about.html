About the DTIS Research Lab

The Digital Trust, Identity and Security (DTIS) Research Lab focuses on building rigorous foundations and practical solutions for secure digital infrastructures. Led by Dr. Saqib Hakak, our group brings together multidisciplinary graduate researchers dedicated to solving emerging challenges in cybersecurity, cryptography, and identity management.

Our Mission

To pioneer resilient frameworks that empower individuals and organizations to trust digital interactions, secure decentralized systems, and protect private data against evolving threat vectors.

Open Science

We believe in reproducible research, open-source codebases, and active collaboration with academic partners, industry leaders, and standard-setting bodies globally.
